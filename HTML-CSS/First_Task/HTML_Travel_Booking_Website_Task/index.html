Welcome to Istanbul, Turkey

Istanbul is the only city in the world that sits on two continents — Europe and Asia — divided by the shimmering Bosphorus Strait. It is a place where ancient history and modern life collide in the most beautiful way.

Why Visit Istanbul?

From the Hagia Sophia to the Grand Bazaar, Istanbul offers something for every traveler. Prices for a 7-day guided tour start as low as $1,200 $899 per person this season.

Quick Facts

Population: approximately 15.5 million
Average summer temperature: 28°C (H2O humidity typically high)
Best time to visit: April–May or September–October*

*Based on average seasonal weather data.

Tip: Bring comfortable walking shoes — most historic sites are best explored on foot.
